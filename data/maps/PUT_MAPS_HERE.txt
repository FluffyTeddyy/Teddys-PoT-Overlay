Put one full-size map image per game map here, e.g.

    gondwa.png
    panjura.png
    isla_spiro.png

The file name (without extension) is the map's name in the tray menu and in
data/quests/<name>.json.

Best source: screenshots of the in-game map screen stitched together, so the
colours and style match the minimap as closely as possible. Bigger and sharper
is better; the app scales it down to 4096 px for matching (map_work_max_dim).
